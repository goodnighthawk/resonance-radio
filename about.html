About Resonance Radio

Published September 27, 2026 · Resonance Radio

Resonance Radio is an independent technical project about low-cost radio, electronics, antennas and audio.

What we publish

Build guides, measurement methods, troubleshooting notes and product tests. The editorial rule is simple: distinguish measured results from seller claims, explain test conditions, and say when something has not been tested yet.

How recommendations work

Price and availability matter, but they do not override function or safety. A product is not described as tested until we have actually tested it. Early build guides may identify candidate parts and clearly label them as such.

Commercial independence

Resonance Radio participates in the Amazon Associates Program. Affiliate relationships do not change the price a reader pays. Commission-bearing links are disclosed clearly, and technical conclusions remain separate from commission rates.

Corrections

If a specification, link or technical statement is wrong, open an issue through the Contact page. Significant corrections will be made in the article rather than hidden.
