BUILD GUIDE

Build a complete AM station for under $100

The goal is not maximum RF power. It is a small, stable, reproducible station that can play automated audio from a computer and be received on an ordinary AM radio.

Architecture
Ubuntu PC → 3.5 mm audio → AM transmitter → matched short antenna → portable AM receiver

What you do not need at first

You do not need a dedicated mixer, studio microphone, expensive antenna tuner or separate playout computer. Start with prerecorded audio from the computer. Add live audio hardware only after the RF chain works cleanly.

U.S. compliance note

For an unlicensed U.S. AM build, design around the applicable FCC Part 15 requirements rather than the transmitter seller’s maximum-power claims. In particular, §15.219 limits final-stage DC input power and the combined antenna/transmission-line/ground-lead length. The transmitter should be characterized and adjusted accordingly before operating it as a station. See our Part 15 AM basics and measurement guide.

First tests

  1. Power the transmitter with no antenna and verify clean audio at low modulation.
  2. Choose an unused local AM frequency.
  3. Connect a short legal-limit antenna system and tune conservatively.
  4. Listen on the portable radio at several distances.
  5. Inspect the spectrum with an SDR before increasing modulation or changing antenna matching.
