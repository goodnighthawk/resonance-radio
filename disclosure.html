DISCLOSURE

Affiliate & editorial disclosure

Published September 27, 2026 · Resonance Radio

Resonance Radio is being prepared for affiliate participation, but commission-bearing Amazon links are not active on the site yet.

Current status

Product links are ordinary links unless they are explicitly identified as affiliate or paid links. We will not describe a normal link as commission-bearing when it is not.

When Amazon Associates links are activated

Pages containing Amazon Special Links will carry a clear link-level disclosure near those recommendations. The site will also display Amazon's required Associates identification statement once the program is active.

Editorial policy

Affiliate commission rate is not a test result. Reviews will state whether a product was purchased, borrowed or supplied, and whether claims are measurements, observations or manufacturer specifications.

Amazon requires clear, conspicuous disclosure near affiliate links and an Associates identification statement on participating sites. See Amazon Associates disclosure guidance.
