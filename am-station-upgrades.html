UPGRADE PATH

The first five upgrades after the sub-$100 AM station works

Published September 27, 2026 · Resonance Radio

A good upgrade fixes a measured limitation. It should not merely make the parts list longer.

1. Add an SDR diagnostic receiver

This is the highest-value upgrade because it exposes carrier drift, harmonics, modulation width and local interference. It improves every later experiment.

2. Improve the loading network

Measure coil resistance and build a more repeatable tuner. A mechanically stable inductor and capacitor arrangement makes comparisons far easier.

3. Clean up the power supply

If the baseline station has audible switching noise, compare supplies or add appropriate filtering. Do this only after confirming the supply is actually the noise source.

4. Add proper audio level control

A simple interface or mixer becomes worthwhile when switching among microphone, prerecorded audio and multiple computers. Until then, software volume is adequate.

5. Improve mechanical construction

Move from loose bench wiring into a labelled enclosure with strain relief, accessible test points and a repeatable antenna connector. Mechanical repeatability is part of measurement quality.

Upgrade rule

Before buying anything, write down the problem it is supposed to solve and the measurement that will tell you whether it worked.

What comes later

Once the station is stable, the project can expand into receiver comparisons, antenna-coil experiments, audio processing and automated programming. Each upgrade then becomes a standalone experiment rather than an arbitrary shopping recommendation.

A sensible spending order

If the first station is clean and stable, spend the next dollars on measurement capability before cosmetic upgrades. An SDR or better test fixture can explain why the system behaves as it does; a nicer enclosure cannot. After measurement comes repeatability: tuner mechanics, labelled connectors and known power supplies. Audio conveniences and presentation hardware come later. This keeps each purchase tied to a question the existing station cannot answer.
